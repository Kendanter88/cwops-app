the station has 4 feed lines
they are for 40 30 20 es 10 meters
the antenna switch is mounted on the mast
just below the antenna
all 4 feed lines connect to the switch
there are 2 long runs of coax go from the switch to the house
one line goes to a k3
the other line goes to the kx3
when either radio has a band change
a band decoder detects it
and sends the band info
over a local net to the antenna switch
to a band pass filter system
the antenna switch and band pass filter
follow the band change automatically.
